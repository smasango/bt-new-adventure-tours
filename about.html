Our Story

Discover South Africa Your Way

BT New Adventure Tours is focused on helping travellers experience South Africa through tailored tours and travel services.

We understand that every traveller is unique, with different interests, schedules and budgets. That's why we don't offer one-size-fits-all packages. Instead, we listen to what you want to experience and work with you to create a customised South African journey.

Whether you're interested in wildlife safaris, historical sites, cultural experiences, adventure activities or simply relaxing in beautiful destinations, we help bring your South African travel dreams to life.
